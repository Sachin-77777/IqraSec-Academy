Threat Intelligence Note - Campaign "SILENT HARBOR"
Classification: TLP:AMBER - synthetic sample data, safe to share
Author: Sachin Shrestha
Date: 2026-10-27

Executive summary
-----------------
Between 12 and 24 October 2026 we tracked a credential-phishing campaign that
delivered a second-stage loader from a look-alike web host. Victims received an
invoice-themed e-mail and were asked to "verify" their mailbox before the
attachment would open.

Delivery and command-and-control
--------------------------------
The phishing page was served from http://secure-login.example-bad.test/verify.
The loader was fetched from http://malware-drop.example-bad.test/payload/stage2.bin.
A fallback channel polled https://cdn-login.example-bad.test/updates/gate.php
every 15 minutes, and the same fallback URL was also seen from a second victim.
Both hosts are fronted by update-server.example-bad.test, which rotated its
address twice during the campaign. The registrar record for
update-server.example-bad.test is the strongest pivot we currently have.

Vulnerabilities abused
----------------------
Initial access chained CVE-2021-44228 (Log4Shell) with a weak credential dump.
CVE-2021-44228 was reused against a second victim one week later.
A third victim was compromised through CVE-2023-34362 (MOVEit Transfer).
